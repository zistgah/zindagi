# Contract — zistgah/zindagi

zindagi is the *Geometry of Living* poster collection, a book built on the **zistgah/kitab**
template. This overlay only ADDS constraints to the master Zistgah CONTRACT
(github.com/zistgah/governance); it never relaxes it.

- **Derived from kitab.** Plumbing (js/, readers/, index.html, assets/theme/) is kitab's and
  is not modified here. Only data changes: config/, content/, assets/figures/, metadata/, and
  these overlays. A build that edits plumbing fails this contract.
- **Posters CC-BY-SA-4.0.** The eighteen posters and chapter text are licensed CC-BY-SA-4.0
  (config/book.config.json meta.license). Metadata CC0-1.0. Canonical DOI metadata lives in
  metadata/misty.json; Zenodo/DataCite/CFF forms are produced by misty, never hand-written.
- **Affiliation is AyeAI only** — never "Independent Researcher". ORCID 0009-0002-0684-8320
  travels with every minted record. ISBN 978-81-985634-0-7.
- **Homepage serves from repo root** (index.html at Pages root, kitab convention).
- **One poster = one chapter.** Eighteen chapters, named by poster title, in the collection's
  order. The book cover is the cover asset, not a chapter.
- **Framing.** The Geometry of Living is presented as an integrative research proposal, not
  established consensus.
- Litigation-simulation hook (C34) is kitab's default stub → zistgah/vakil; learning links →
  zistgah/varzish. Both kept as kitab defaults.

Reconstruct faithfully. Critique with evidence, not tone. Maintain contract and context.
